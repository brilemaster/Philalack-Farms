# Philalack Farms: Family Game Guide

Version 0.40 · Philalack Farms, Levels 1–3 · Nov 14, 2026

## Who's who

All seven are playable, picked on the home screen in this order: **Dad, Mom, Jake (14), Nolan (12), Leo (6), Logan (8, cousin), Lylah (2, cousin)**.

- **Buttons** the goat follows you with **Dill** the armadillo riding on his back. The HEADBUTT button sends Buttons charging at the closest goose, pumpkin, loose hay or Henry, on either side.
- **Dill** digs burrow shortcuts and secret tunnels.
- **Henry** is the runaway cow. Happy, goofy, and never caught.
- **Saddled geese** can be ridden for a short time.

Henry is the family's real cow: a boy, fully black. Outfits, Logan's and Lylah's hair, and the names Buttons and Dill are placeholders until the family's intake answers come in.

## Look and tone

Modern pixel art (classic-platformer feel, original characters), bright colors, crisp modern text. Kid-friendly slapstick with jokes for the parents.

## The running story

Henry escapes at the end of every level in a new silly way, and the family follows him. One new cow joins per chapter.

**Chapter 1: Fall**
1. Pumpkin Patch: built. Ends with a rodeo: jump on Henry, tap JUMP each time he bucks (3 bucks per ride, 3 rides), then he escapes in a giant pumpkin.
2. Hayloft Heights: built (reworked as a climb in v0.20). Climb seven loft floors from the barn floor to the hay ring at the top, then Henry slides down the hay chute and out the barn doors toward the creek.
3. Creek Crossing: built (v0.30). Rocks, floating logs, jumping fish, a waterfall secret and a raft chase. Henry ditches his raft and runs into the corn.
4. Corn Maze: next
5. Smokehouse Showdown: Henry wrecks Dad's smoker (the true story). Teaser for Winter.

## One world, many levels (v0.18)

The whole game is one world, Philalack Farms. Each stop on the farm map is a level, and new levels get added to the map over time.

## The farm map

Play opens the Chapter 1 farm map. Each stop shows a checkmark when cleared and its 3 golden egg slots. A level unlocks when the one before it is cleared; locked stops show a padlock, and Henry peeks out from the next locked one. Tap any open stop to play or replay it, or use the big ◀ ▶ arrows in the bottom left to step between levels. After clearing a level: **Next level ▶** or **🗺 Farm map**.

**Home screen:** ◀ ▶ arrows on either side of the player cards switch characters (tapping a card still works). **New Game** starts Level 1 with the opening story; it never erases unlocked levels, golden eggs or Mom's purse. **🗺 Map** opens the farm map on the level you left off at (the line under it says where).

On the map, a level where you reached a scarecrow shows **▶ Continue**, which picks up at that scarecrow, plus a smaller **↺ Start over**. Locked levels show a padlock even before you've beaten anything.

## Level 2: Hayloft Heights (a climbing level)

- Starts on the barn floor. A hen hints that the ladder up is hidden behind loose hay; Buttons headbutts it away
- **Ladders:** a blue ⬆ CLIMB button appears when you're at a ladder. Hold it to climb; ⬇ climbs down; JUMP hops off
- Seven loft floors to climb, each with a different way up: hay-bale steps, ladders and red-striped **springy bales** (hold jump to bounce higher). The camera rises with you and leans to show more above or below
- **Falling:** a missed jump drops you one floor, no harm done. On Normal and Hard a few gaps drop you two floors; that costs a heart and puts you back where you jumped from. Easy never costs a heart
- Rolling hay bales come down some floors; geese patrol the catwalks
- **Hay ring** at the very top: a lasso showdown instead of a ride. Get Henry dizzy (he bonks a wall, or Buttons headbutts him), get close and tap 🤠 LASSO (the headbutt button changes), then tap JUMP fast to win the tug-of-war. Win 3 tugs. Hay falls from the rafters each time he bonks a wall. Then he busts through the wall, slides down the hay chute and runs out the barn doors toward the creek
- **Secret hen roost** (busy): a door behind loose hay on floor 5. Egg shower and a golden egg
- **Secret grain bin** (relaxed): Dill's burrow on floor 4. Quiet room with a golden egg, a treasure chest and Mom's sunglasses
- Third golden egg floats over a gap on floor 5
- Scarecrows on floors 1, 4 and 7

## Level 3: Creek Crossing

- Creeks to cross: hop the stepping stones, ride the floating logs, or take the saddled goose over the wide river (a golden egg floats high over it)
- Falling in the water works like a pit: back to where you jumped from, and a heart on Normal and Hard
- Jumping fish leap out of the water. Stomp them or let Buttons bonk them
- **Behind the waterfall** (busy): climb the rock ledge and walk right into the falls. Egg shower and a golden egg
- **Secret fishing shack** (relaxed): Dill's burrow on top of the rocky hill. Quiet room with a golden egg, a treasure chest and Dad's Lucky Lure
- Dill's tunnel: Nolan's Sneaker and Logan's Toy Truck. The purse now holds 13
- **Showdown: the raft chase.** Henry floats off on a raft and the screen scrolls on its own. Hop rocks, islands and logs to keep up, and land on his raft 3 times. Falling in puts you back on the nearest rock (costs a heart on Normal and Hard). Then he ditches the raft and runs into the corn maze

## No boss fights (v0.40)

Levels end when you reach the end of the path (hoofprints and a ➜ mark the spot). The level-complete screen gives a short note on where Henry escaped to next. The levels are longer to make up for it:
- Level 1 continues past the old pen into the back forty: more pumpkins, geese, crates, a plank crossing over a gap and a second saddled goose
- Level 2 adds the rafters at the very top; the finish is jumping into the hay chute, sliding all the way down and running out the barn doors
- Level 3 keeps the old chase stretch as a regular (no auto-scroll) finale across rocks, islands and logs to the corn maze

The rodeo, lasso and raft-chase code is kept and can come back for a chapter finale.

## Look

Chunky squared game font (Russo One), gold/silver/bronze/diamond eggs in the egg showers, dark HUD-style hint tags, no floating pop-up text, darker outlines.

## How it plays

- Fast running with a turbo scurry after a moment, punchy jumps, hold jump to keep bouncing
- Eggs with combo streaks, multi-egg crates, a hidden crate, stomp chains for bonus eggs
- 3 golden eggs: one high in the sky, one in each secret room
- 2 secret rooms: a chicken coop behind loose hay (headbutt it) and a treasure cave through a hidden burrow up high. Each has a rainbow egg shower (big the first visit, smaller on revisits)
- Dill's tunnel: travel underground past buried junk at a cruising pace, in either direction, as often as you like. Walk over the tunnel later to see what's buried
- **Mom's Purse** collection: 6 funny farm finds (5 buried in Dill's tunnel, a rubber duck in the treasure cave). Collected items fly into the 👜 button at the top, which opens a menu of everything found. Saved on the device
- Golden eggs glow with spinning light rays and sparkles
- Burrows and the coop door: walk onto one to drop right in. To leave a secret room, stand on the exit and press ⬇ (DOWN). Secret rooms are wider, with the exit at the far right
- Home screen: ◀ ▶ arrows to switch characters, ▶ New Game (starts Level 1 with the skippable story) and 🗺 Map (opens on where you left off)
- Cowboy hat power-up, heart crates, scarecrow checkpoints
- Easy (no pits, bumps don't cost hearts), Normal (5 hearts), Hard (3 hearts, faster Henry)
- Progress saves on the device only

## Commercial-safety check (v0.16)

All characters, art, music and sound effects are original and made in code. No tubes or pipes (burrows), no question-mark blocks (wooden egg crates), no flagpole (rodeo finish), no mushroom-style power-ups (cowboy hat), no red cap and overalls. No outside fonts, images or scripts. Never market it as "Mario-style"; say "inspired by classic platform games."
